PPlainly Checking on-device AI…

Understand any document.
Keep your details to yourself.

Snap a bank letter, a lab report or a rental agreement. Plainly explains it in plain words, using Gemini Nano on your own device. If you want a translation or a deeper answer, the cloud only ever sees a copy with your personal details hidden.

or
Nothing leaves your device unless you ask for the cloud.
On-device AI isn’t ready in this browser.

Plainly uses Gemini Nano in Chrome 139+ on desktop. Turn on chrome://flags/#prompt-api-for-gemini-nano-multimodal-input, restart Chrome, then download the model (about 4 GB, one time).

Without it, Plainly still hides personal details with its built-in rules and asks Gemini in the cloud using only the redacted copy.

Translations into Indian languages and deeper questions go to Gemini in the cloud with the redacted copy.
